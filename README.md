# EX.NO.10

## CONTENT CREATION USING PROMPT PATTERNS

**Date:** 05-09-2026
**Reg. No.:** 212224040055

## AIM

To demonstrate how various prompting techniques such as **Query Decomposition, Decision Making, Answer Engineering, Fact Check List, Tail Generation, Menu Actions, and Semantic Filtering** can be used with ChatGPT to create high-quality content such as reports and articles. The experiment also demonstrates how refining prompts improves the **quality, coherence, accuracy, structure, and relevance** of generated content.

## PROCEDURE

1. Understand different prompt patterns used for content generation:

   * **Query Decomposition:** Breaking a complex task into smaller sub-tasks.
   * **Decision Making:** Asking the AI to select the most suitable option.
   * **Answer Engineering:** Providing specific instructions about structure and format.
   * **Fact Check List:** Asking the AI to identify and verify important factual claims.
   * **Tail Generation:** Extending existing content logically.
   * **Menu Actions:** Providing multiple choices and asking the AI to select an appropriate action.
   * **Semantic Filter:** Controlling the tone, style, relevance, and level of detail.

2. Select suitable content-generation scenarios.

3. Begin with a simple prompt to generate a basic first draft.

4. Refine the prompt by adding requirements such as structure, audience, tone, length, and factual considerations.

5. Apply different prompt patterns to improve the generated content.

6. Generate multiple versions and compare their quality.

7. Evaluate the generated content based on:

   * Coherence
   * Creativity
   * Accuracy
   * Relevance
   * Tone and Style
   * Structure

8. Select and polish the best version as the final output.

# TEST CASE 1: BUSINESS REPORT ON MARKET TRENDS

## Topic

**Current Trends in the E-Commerce Industry**

### Step 1 – Basic Prompt

**Prompt:**

"Write a report about current trends in the e-commerce industry."

### First Draft

E-commerce has become an important part of modern business. Customers increasingly use online platforms to purchase products and services. Some major trends in e-commerce include mobile shopping, digital payments, artificial intelligence, personalized recommendations, social commerce, and faster delivery services.

Businesses are using technology to understand customer preferences and improve their services. Artificial intelligence helps companies recommend products and automate customer support. Digital payment systems have also made online transactions easier.

The future of e-commerce is expected to focus on personalization, convenience, automation, and improved customer experience.

---

## Step 2 – Query Decomposition

**Prompt:**

"Analyze the e-commerce industry by breaking the task into these sections: major market trends, technology adoption, customer behaviour, digital payments, challenges, and future opportunities. Then prepare a structured report."

### Refined Output

### 1. Major Market Trends

The e-commerce industry is increasingly influenced by mobile commerce, social commerce, personalized shopping, and faster delivery services.

### 2. Technology Adoption

Artificial intelligence, machine learning, cloud computing, and data analytics are being used to improve recommendations, customer service, inventory management, and business decisions.

### 3. Customer Behaviour

Customers increasingly expect convenient shopping, competitive prices, personalized recommendations, multiple payment options, and quick delivery.

### 4. Digital Payments

Digital wallets, UPI, cards, and other electronic payment methods have made online transactions faster and more convenient.

### 5. Challenges

Major challenges include cybersecurity, data privacy, intense competition, logistics, return management, and maintaining customer trust.

### 6. Future Opportunities

Businesses can benefit from AI-based personalization, automation, sustainable packaging, improved logistics, and omnichannel shopping experiences.

---

## Step 3 – Decision-Making Prompt

**Prompt:**

"Choose the three most important trends likely to influence e-commerce in the near future from the following: AI personalization, digital payments, social commerce, faster delivery, augmented reality, and sustainable practices. Explain why you selected them."

### Output

The three most important trends are:

1. **AI Personalization** – It can help businesses provide product recommendations and customized experiences.
2. **Digital Payments** – Easy and secure payments improve the convenience of online shopping.
3. **Faster Delivery** – Customers increasingly value speed and convenience when purchasing online.

---

## Step 4 – Answer Engineering

**Prompt:**

"Create a professional business report on e-commerce trends. Use the headings Introduction, Key Trends, Benefits, Challenges, Future Opportunities, and Conclusion. Use formal language, concise paragraphs, and bullet points where appropriate."

### Improved Version

### Introduction

E-commerce has transformed the way businesses sell products and consumers purchase goods and services. Increasing internet access, smartphones, digital payments, and technological innovation continue to influence the industry.

### Key Trends

* Artificial intelligence and personalized recommendations
* Mobile and social commerce
* Digital payment systems
* Faster and flexible delivery
* Data-driven business decisions
* Sustainable e-commerce practices

### Benefits

These developments improve customer convenience, operational efficiency, product discovery, and business decision-making.

### Challenges

Businesses must address cybersecurity threats, data privacy, logistics, customer retention, and increasing competition.

### Future Opportunities

AI-driven services, automation, personalized experiences, and improved logistics can create new opportunities for e-commerce companies.

### Conclusion

The e-commerce industry is continuously evolving through technology and changing customer expectations. Businesses that effectively adopt emerging technologies while maintaining security and customer trust can achieve long-term growth.

---

## Step 5 – Fact Check List

**Prompt:**

"Create a fact-check checklist for the above e-commerce report. Identify claims that require verification and separate general observations from claims requiring current data."

### Fact Check Output

| Claim                                              | Verification Required |
| -------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| E-commerce is growing globally                     | Yes                   |
| AI is used for product recommendations             | Yes                   |
| Digital payments support online transactions       | Yes                   |
| Customers value convenience and faster delivery    | Yes                   |
| Cybersecurity is an important e-commerce challenge | Yes                   |
| AI and automation can create future opportunities  | Yes                   |

**Observation:** Current statistics, market size, growth rates, and company-specific information should be verified using reliable sources before being used in a formal business report.

---

## Step 6 – Semantic Filter

**Prompt:**

"Rewrite the report using a formal, professional, and concise business tone. Remove unnecessary repetition and keep the content suitable for a college-level report."

### Final Version

**E-Commerce Industry: Emerging Market Trends**

E-commerce has become a major component of modern business, supported by widespread internet access, smartphones, digital payments, and technological innovation. Businesses are increasingly adopting digital platforms to improve customer experience and operational efficiency.

Important trends include **artificial intelligence-based personalization, mobile commerce, social commerce, digital payments, faster delivery, and data-driven decision-making**. AI can help businesses analyze customer preferences and provide personalized recommendations. Digital payment systems provide convenient methods for completing online transactions.

However, the industry also faces challenges such as cybersecurity, data privacy, intense competition, logistics, and customer retention. Companies must continuously improve their services while maintaining customer trust.

In the future, AI, automation, personalization, and sustainable business practices are expected to provide significant opportunities. Businesses that successfully combine technology with customer-focused strategies can strengthen their position in the competitive e-commerce market.

# TEST CASE 2: ARTICLE ON CLIMATE CHANGE

## Topic

**Impact of Climate Change on the Environment and Society**

### Step 1 – Basic Prompt

**Prompt:**

"Write an article about climate change."

### First Draft

Climate change refers to long-term changes in temperature and weather patterns. Human activities such as burning fossil fuels contribute significantly to the increase of greenhouse gases in the atmosphere.

Climate change can affect ecosystems, agriculture, water resources, and human communities. Rising temperatures and changing rainfall patterns may create difficulties for agriculture and water management.

Reducing greenhouse gas emissions, increasing renewable energy use, protecting forests, and promoting sustainable practices are important steps toward addressing climate change.

---

## Step 2 – Query Decomposition

**Prompt:**

"Write an article about climate change by dividing it into causes, environmental impacts, effects on society, effects on agriculture, solutions, and individual responsibilities."

### Refined Output

### Causes

* Burning of fossil fuels
* Deforestation
* Industrial activities
* Transportation
* Changes in land use

### Environmental Impacts

* Rising temperatures
* Changes in rainfall patterns
* Melting glaciers and ice
* Sea-level rise
* Ecosystem disruption

### Effects on Society

Climate-related changes can affect communities through extreme weather events, resource pressures, health risks, and economic impacts.

### Effects on Agriculture

Changes in temperature and rainfall can influence crop productivity, water availability, and agricultural planning.

### Solutions

* Renewable energy
* Energy efficiency
* Forest conservation
* Sustainable transportation
* Waste reduction

### Individual Responsibility

Individuals can reduce energy consumption, avoid unnecessary waste, use public transportation where practical, and support environmentally responsible practices.

---

## Step 3 – Menu Actions

**Prompt:**

"Choose the best format for communicating climate change to college students from these options: (A) technical research report, (B) simple educational article, (C) fictional story, (D) advertisement. Select one and explain why."

### Output

**Selected option: B – Simple Educational Article**

A simple educational article is most appropriate because it can explain climate change clearly while remaining informative and accessible to college students.

---

## Step 4 – Tail Generation

**Prompt:**

"Continue the climate change article by adding a section about how students can contribute to environmental sustainability. Maintain the same educational tone."

### Output

Students can contribute to environmental sustainability through everyday choices. They can reduce unnecessary electricity consumption, minimize waste, use reusable products, conserve water, participate in tree-planting activities, and encourage sustainable practices in their communities. Educational institutions can also promote environmental awareness through campaigns, projects, and practical sustainability initiatives.

---

## Step 5 – Semantic Filter

**Prompt:**

"Rewrite the complete article in a clear, educational, and neutral tone. Avoid exaggerated statements, unnecessary technical terminology, and repetition. Use headings and concise paragraphs."

### Final Version

## Climate Change: Impact on the Environment and Society

### Introduction

Climate change refers to long-term changes in Earth's climate patterns. Human activities, particularly the release of greenhouse gases from activities such as burning fossil fuels, have contributed significantly to recent warming.

### Major Causes

The major contributors include fossil fuel consumption, deforestation, industrial processes, transportation, and changes in land use. These activities can increase the concentration of greenhouse gases in the atmosphere.

### Environmental Impact

Climate change can affect ecosystems, temperature patterns, rainfall, water availability, and biodiversity. Changes in environmental conditions can place pressure on plants and animals and alter natural habitats.

### Impact on Society

Climate-related changes can affect human communities through extreme weather events, changes in water availability, health risks, infrastructure damage, and economic pressures. The effects can vary between regions and communities.

### Impact on Agriculture

Agriculture depends strongly on suitable temperature, rainfall, soil conditions, and water availability. Changes in these factors can create challenges for crop production and agricultural planning.

### Solutions

Climate change can be addressed through a combination of mitigation and adaptation measures. Important approaches include increasing renewable energy use, improving energy efficiency, protecting forests, reducing emissions, improving sustainable transportation, and developing climate-resilient systems.

### Role of Students

Students can contribute by conserving energy and water, reducing waste, using reusable materials, participating in environmental activities, and spreading awareness about sustainable practices.

### Conclusion

Climate change is an important environmental and social challenge. Addressing it requires cooperation among governments, industries, communities, educational institutions, and individuals. Awareness, technological innovation, sustainable practices, and responsible decision-making can contribute to a more resilient future.

# RESULT

Two content-generation scenarios were successfully created using different prompt patterns. The experiment demonstrated that **structured and iterative prompting produces better content than simple prompts alone**. Query decomposition improved organization, decision-making helped select suitable content, answer engineering improved formatting, fact-checking improved reliability, tail generation added depth, and semantic filtering improved tone and relevance.

# CONCLUSION

This experiment demonstrated the effective use of different prompt patterns for AI-based content creation. Starting with a simple prompt and gradually refining it produced more **structured, coherent, relevant, and high-quality content**. The experiment also showed that prompt engineering can be applied to different content types such as business reports, educational articles, case studies, and creative writing. Therefore, effective prompt design plays an important role in obtaining useful and well-organized outputs from AI language models.
